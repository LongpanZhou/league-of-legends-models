# league-of-legends-models

League of Legends 3D models in glTF binary (`.glb`), for the Summoner's Rift scene on
[longpanzhou.github.io](https://longpanzhou.github.io).

- `models/` the map's structures and monsters, and `models/champions/` the champions
- `thumbs/` a small picture of each model
- `models.json` the list of everything, with sizes and pictures

The site reads `models.json` for its editor and downloads a model from here when someone drops it on the
map. Files are served through jsDelivr (`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/LongpanZhou/league-of-legends-models@main/`).

League of Legends and its characters and models belong to Riot Games.
